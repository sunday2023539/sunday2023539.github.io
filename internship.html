Internship

Visualization and deformation-based analysis of ensemble coastal flood data for the Arcachon Basin.

Internship at IGN/LASTIG

During my internship at IGN/LASTIG/ENSG-Géomatique in Paris, France, I worked on the visualization and analysis of ensemble coastal flood data for the Arcachon Basin. My work focused on flood exposure mapping, synthetic shoreline-based representation, polar-radial deformation, mixed-scale transformation and cartographic styling.

Host Organization: IGN / LASTIG / GeodataParis, Paris, France
Internship Focus: Coastal flood visualization and map deformation
Role: Geovisualization and Spatial Analysis Intern
Tools: Python, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Numpy, Shapely, Geopandas

Internship Focus

Flood Exposure Mapping

I processed and visualized ensemble coastal flood data, population and infrastucture to identify exposed coastal zones around the Arcachon Basin.

Polar-Radial Deformation

I developed and tested a polar-radial deformation workflow which allows me to transform a curved coastal corridor into a normalized strip, where flood occurrence and exposed features could be compare along the coastline.

Mixed-Scale Transformation

I used the a focus-and-context approach to enlarge high-risk hotspots for better visibility of infrastructures while compressing safer areas to keep the overall map continuous.

Selected Outputs

Deformation Flood Corridor

This output shows the maximum/outer boundary and minimum boundary of the deformation space. the clean control boundaries allows us to explicitly emphasize the specific geographic areas of interest.

Polar-Radial Deformation

This output shows an experimental polar-radial transformation of the map from normal geographic space into polar space.

Mixed-Scale Transformation

To effectively communicate the data, a focus-and-context cartographic design was applied with appropriate cartographic styling.
